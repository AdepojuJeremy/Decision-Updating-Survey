# Figures

Figure 1 introduces decision updating through illustrative scenarios. Figure 2 connects incoming evidence to decision updating and possible model behaviours. Figure 3 maps the three primary evidence categories and the explicitly orthogonal synthesis branch.

The JPG previews of Figures 1 and 2 are AI-generated illustrations created during manuscript development. Their draw.io files are editable vector recreations; individual illustration details may differ. Figure 3 is programmatically typeset vector artwork.

Open the files in `editable/` using diagrams.net. All diagrams contain individually editable shapes, text, and connectors. Figure 3 uses bright branch colours, straight connectors, and a bottom legend. Its PDF and SVG retain vector text. The complete tree is dense; use `figure-3-panels.pdf` for larger branch views.

## Figure 3 legend

- **§** refers to the main text; lettered references point to appendix subsections.
- **PRE** marks preparatory training or calibration.
- **POST** marks evidence-time inference or response processing.
- Both ribbons indicate combined stages.

The timing annotations are the survey's synthesis. Related component methods are not presented as evidence of complete decision-updating competence. Branches are conceptual relationships, not measured quantities.

The visual hierarchy and timing-marker idea are adapted from Xu et al., [Knowledge Conflicts for LLMs: A Survey](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.486/), Figure 3. Include this attribution in the manuscript caption or surrounding text; it is intentionally kept outside the figure artwork.

Figure 3's author-year suffixes belong to its standalone bibliography and should be harmonized with the final manuscript bibliography during typesetting. Bibliographic editions in the repository catalogue are identified separately.
